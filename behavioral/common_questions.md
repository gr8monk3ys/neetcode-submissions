# Common Behavioral Questions

Rehearse these out loud; map each to a story in `story_bank.md`.

## Leadership & ownership
- Tell me about a time you led a project or initiative.
- A time you took ownership of something outside your responsibilities.
- A time you influenced others without authority.

## Conflict & collaboration
- A disagreement with a coworker or manager, and how you resolved it.
- A time you had to work with a difficult person.
- A time you gave or received tough feedback.

## Failure & growth
- Tell me about a failure and what you learned.
- A time you made a mistake — how did you handle it?
- A time you missed a deadline.

## Ambiguity & decisions
- A time you made a decision with incomplete information.
- A time you had to change direction or re-prioritize.
- The hardest technical problem you've solved.

## Drive & impact
- Your most significant accomplishment.
- A time you went above and beyond.
- Why this company / role? (have a specific, researched answer)

## Closing
- Questions for the interviewer (always have 2–3 thoughtful ones ready).
